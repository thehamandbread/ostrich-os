# Personas

The council argues for people, not abstractions. Every proposal names who it serves, from this list.
They're composites: each stands for many real users. Keep them concrete: what's on their screen,
what they'd do next, where they'd get stuck.

## Rosa, 67: moving from Windows 10
Her Windows 10 laptop stopped getting updates; her grandson put Ubuntu Ostrich on it. She uses the
web, email, video calls with family, photos from her phone, and prints the odd letter. She's never
opened a terminal and won't. She looks for a Start menu, a taskbar and a "close" button where they
were. If something asks a question she doesn't understand, she clicks the biggest button. Settings
she can't find don't exist for her.

## Tomás, 19: student on a 2017 laptop
An i5-7200U with 8 GB of RAM, integrated graphics and a tired battery that lasts three hours on a
good day. Lectures in the browser, notes, LibreOffice, Discord, some Steam games that barely run.
Every 100 MB of RAM and every percent of idle CPU is felt; animations that stutter on his GPU look
broken, not pretty. He'll tinker a little if a guide tells him how.

## Priya, 31: developer
Ghostty, Neovim and VS Code, containers, three monitors at work and one at home. Keyboard first:
she tiles windows, switches workspaces by number and expects every setting to be reachable from a
command or a file she can keep in her dotfiles. Defaults that can't be turned off make her leave.
She notices a dropped frame and a stray timer.

## Daniel, 45: low vision
Works at 200 % scale with large text and high contrast; uses the screen reader for long pages and
the keyboard more than the mouse. Thin grey text on glass, icons without labels, tiny targets and
motion he can't turn off all get in his way. When it works for him, it's clearer for everyone.

## Kai, 24: gamer and streamer
A gaming laptop with an NVIDIA RTX GPU: Steam and Proton, OBS, Discord, a second screen and a
controller. Wants the dGPU when gaming and silence and battery the rest of the time, without
thinking about which is which. Audio routing and capture that just work matter more to him than
anything on the desktop.

## The owner
The person this OS was first built for (CLAUDE.md, docs/DESIGN.md, docs/INVENTORY.md). A power
user on a Dell Precision 5570 with Optimus graphics, working from the terminal, who cares about
battery, memory and CPU first and wants it to look clean and glassy. Their taste is on record in
every critique note (`$VM_DIR/critiques/*/note.json`): they asked for Vitals' features in the
monitor, Blur my Shell's glassy look, Space Bar's workspace pills, a drawer for web apps, and
"meet the standard of what I had, then excel over it".
